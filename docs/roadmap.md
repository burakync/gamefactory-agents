# Limited future directions

Feature development is frozen for this preview. No dates or commitments are announced. Potential later work: improve the supported manual external-host experience; define representative evaluation tasks; reduce verification cost without weaker guarantees; clarify distribution rights and supported platforms.

Autonomous model providers, self-improvement, payment/activation systems and commercial availability are not included. Star Greenhouse 2 development has stopped. Little Star Jump stays a single-screen demonstration.
